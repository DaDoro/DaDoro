## 👋 Hi

I'm 19 and have been programming since I was 11: I started with C# and Unity at a programming school, and these days I mostly write Python.

Most of my projects start the same way: my friends or I are missing something — an easy way to watch movies together, believable traffic on our Assetto Corsa server, automatic tax receipts for my own service. Some of those projects grow into products with real users.

I build in a pair with Claude Code: I own the architecture, review and debugging, and hand the routine to AI.
